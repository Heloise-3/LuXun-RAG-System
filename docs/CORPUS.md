# Corpus notes

The active corpus has 14 entries: 11 continuous excerpts retained from eight of 30 supplied quotation candidates, plus the project's three original passages. Twenty-two supplied candidates were excluded because they substantially rewrote the original, mixed incompatible quotations or lacked traceable attribution.

The 11 reviewed excerpts are marked `verified` only in the sense that their text was checked against the linked digital source. This does not certify the supplied 1981 print-edition page references. Those references remain unverified. Script conversion, quotation marks and explicitly described character normalization are recorded in editorial notes.

The three retained original entries have IDs 1, 2 and 3. Their text and literary tags are unchanged, and their provenance status remains `unverified`. They are included in retrieval; the interface shows their status when they are returned.

All four metadata categories and allowed tag values follow `dictionary.md`. Tags are editorial labels, not measurements of an author's personality. Query analysis is constrained to the same vocabulary; unknown values are rejected rather than silently treated as matching tags.

The current index uses Qwen/Qwen3-Embedding-8B and contains 14 vectors of 4,096 dimensions. Its checksum binds it to the active corpus. The three original passages received new embeddings when restored; the preceding 11 vectors were reused without changes.

Before adding material, preserve a continuous passage, identify the work and record a source URL or edition/location. Avoid treating an AI paraphrase or a stitched quotation as original prose. Programmatic validation checks field completeness and consistency, not textual authenticity.
